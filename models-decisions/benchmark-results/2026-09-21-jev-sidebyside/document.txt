MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT — ACME CORP AND NORTHWIND LOGISTICS

1. TERM. This Agreement commences on 1 March 2026 and continues for thirty-six (36)
months unless terminated earlier under Section 7.

2. FEES. Northwind shall pay Acme USD 42,000 per month, invoiced in arrears on the
first business day of each month. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. SERVICE LEVELS. Acme guarantees 99.95% monthly uptime measured at the load
balancer. Failure to meet this level entitles Northwind to a service credit of 10%
of that month's fees. Service credits are the sole and exclusive remedy for
availability failures.

4. DATA. Northwind retains all right, title and interest in Customer Data. Acme may
process Customer Data solely to provide the services and shall delete it within 30
days of termination. Acme shall not use Customer Data to train machine learning
models.

5. LIABILITY. Each party's aggregate liability is capped at the fees paid in the
twelve (12) months preceding the claim. Neither party is liable for indirect or
consequential damages. This cap does not apply to breaches of Section 4 or to
indemnification obligations under Section 6.

6. INDEMNIFICATION. Acme shall indemnify Northwind against third-party claims that
the services infringe a US patent, provided Northwind gives prompt notice.

7. TERMINATION. Either party may terminate for material breach on 30 days' written
notice if the breach remains uncured. Northwind may terminate for convenience after
month 18 on 90 days' notice, subject to an early termination fee of two months' fees.

8. GOVERNING LAW. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
Disputes shall be resolved by binding arbitration in Wilmington, Delaware.
